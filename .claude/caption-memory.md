## What Works (Approved / Liked)
- "Find people who make you feel like this 🌀" ✅ — relatable, tag-worthy, feeling-first
- "Jalalo Bilalo starts playing and your body just… 🌀" — unfinished sentence, gets comments
- "No therapy. Just Ghoomar. 🌀" — punchy, relatable
- Hooks that sell the *feeling*, not the performance
- Belgium as context (in caption body), not as the lead hook
- Hinglish works when it feels natural, not forced
- "Tag the person you'd spin with 👇" — call to action that fits the content

## What Doesn't Work (Rejected / Too Far)
- Grand statements: "We didn't bring India to Europe. India was already there." — too marketing-speak
- Seeking-validation framing: "Belgium was amazed by us"
- Clever but cold hooks: "Ghoomar. Belgium. Obviously."
- Highlighting gender in the hook — let it be organic
- Hooks that feel like ad copy

---

## Caption Structure (Preferred)
1. **Hook** — feeling-first, relatable, short (1 line)
2. **Body** — context: what, where, who (1–3 lines, natural tone)
3. **CTA** — tag-worthy or comment-inviting ("Tag the person you'd spin with 👇")

## On-Video Text vs Caption Hook
- These must be **different** — different jobs
- **On-video text**: names the *what* (song, event, moment) — visual, immediate
- **Caption hook**: sells the *feeling* — makes them click, share, tag

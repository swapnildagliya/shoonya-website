Interview me about the feature or app I want to build. Ask me focused questions — one or two at a time — until you fully understand:

- The goal: what problem does this solve and for whom?
- The must-have requirements: what must it do to be considered working?
- The constraints: tech stack, time, what must not change?
- The shape: what does the finished thing look like or how is it used?

Do NOT start building. Keep asking until you have enough to write a complete spec.

When you have enough, write a clear, detailed spec and save it to `spec.md`. The spec must include:

1. **Situation** — context and the problem being solved
2. **Requirements** — numbered list of must-have behaviours
3. **Edge cases** — things that could go wrong or behave unexpectedly
4. **Definition of done** — a concrete checklist someone could use to verify the build is complete

Tell me when `spec.md` is written and summarise what it covers.
